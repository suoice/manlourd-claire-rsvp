MESSAGE FROM THE COUPLE

To our beloved family & friends,

Some of the most beautiful stories begin in the simplest ways, and ours began with a little message during our college days. We never imagined that a simple “Hi” would lead us here to a love that has grown through time, distance, challenges, and countless answered prayers.

Our journey has never been perfect, but through every season, we have learned that love is more than just choosing each other on the happiest days. It is choosing to stay, to understand, to forgive, and to keep growing together, even when life gets difficult. Above all, we have learned to place God at the center of our relationship, trusting that His plans are always greater than our own.

Now, as we prepare to begin a new chapter as husband and wife, our hearts are filled with gratitude. We are thankful for the love that brought us this far and for the people who have been part of our story. Your presence, prayers, and blessings mean more to us than words can express.

On December 30, 2026, we will finally say “I do” not because our journey has been perfect, but because we have found in each other a love worth choosing, a home worth building, and a future worth praying for.

We cannot wait to celebrate this beautiful beginning with you. Thank you for being part of our story and for witnessing the day two hearts, guided by faith and held together by love, become one.

With love and grateful hearts,

Manlourd & Claire
